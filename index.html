About Himalay

We make everyday plastic products for the home in Morbi, Gujarat: bathroom sets and buckets, kitchen storage and lunch boxes, water jugs and dispensers, and cleaning essentials.

Every piece is moulded from durable, recyclable thermoplast and checked before it leaves the factory. We supply dealers, distributors and shops, and we welcome new partners.
